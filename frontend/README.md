# 👁️ Vision AI  
### AI-Powered Competitive Intelligence Platform 🚀

> **See competitors clearly. Act faster. Win smarter.**

---

## 🧠 What is Vision AI?
Vision AI is a **unified competitive intelligence tool** that helps startups and businesses understand:
- 🔍 *Why competitors are winning*
- 📉 *Where you are lagging*
- ⚡ *How to outperform them — fast*

No more checking 10 websites and 5 tools manually.

---

## ❌ Problem
Companies struggle to:
- Track competitor pricing 💰
- Analyze SEO performance 📊
- Monitor content & product updates 📝
- Identify strategic gaps 🕳️

Everything is **scattered, slow, and expensive**.

---

## ✅ Solution
**Vision AI brings everything into one dashboard**:
- Competitor tracking
- SEO & content analysis
- Pricing intelligence
- AI-powered gap detection
- Downloadable reports

---

## 🧩 Core Features
- 📊 **Dashboard** – Complete competitor overview  
- 🏷️ **Competitor Manager** – Direct / Indirect / Aspirational  
- 💰 **Price Monitor** – Entry, Mid & Premium tiers  
- 📝 **Content Tracker** – Blogs, pages, frequency  
- 📈 **SEO Advisor** – On-page & Technical scores  
- 🧠 **Gap Finder (AI)** – Find where you can win  
- 🔔 **Product Watcher** – Alerts on changes  
- 📄 **Reports** – Download & share insights  

(Max: **10 competitors per project**)
